### Decision Workflow (Frame → Options → Decide → Communicate)

For making and communicating a call, not producing a document. The decision itself is a human judgment (Rule 5) — the assistant frames the question and lays out options, but never decides.

#### Phase 1 — Frame
State the decision to be made and what a good outcome looks like, in plain terms.

#### Phase 2 — Options
Lay out two or three real choices side by side, with their costs, trade-offs, and risks. Keep conflicting advice visible; do not average it into one bland recommendation (Rule 7).

#### Phase 3 — Decide
Make the call and write down WHY, plus who owns it — so it can be understood and revisited later. This is a human judgment call, not something to delegate to the AI.

#### Phase 4 — Communicate
Share the decision and its reasoning with the people it affects, in the words they will understand.
