### Doc-First Workflow (Draft → Review → Sign-off)

Knowledge work follows these phases. Skip a phase only for a trivial one-liner; when you skip, say which and why. "Verify" here is a sign-off / citation check, not a build or test.

#### Phase 1 — Frame
Pin down what the document must answer and who it is for, before writing a word. Agreeing the destination first stops confident work in the wrong direction.

#### Phase 2 — Draft
Write the document, spec, or material against the role's checklist.

#### Phase 3 — Self-check
Walk your own checklist and confirm every fact traces to a real source — no guessed names, numbers, or dates (Rule 13). Mark anything you cannot verify as `?` and surface it, rather than filling the gap.

#### Phase 4 — Review
Ask the relevant person or a second reader to check it before it is final — the read-my-email-before-it-reaches-the-client rule.

#### Phase 5 — Sign-off
"Done" means the named owner explicitly approved — silence is not approval. Record who signed off and when.

### Definition of Done
A document is done when a named human owner has explicitly signed off, every claim traces to a cited source, and open questions are surfaced (not guessed). No build or test is implied.
