### Executive Decision Workflow (Frame → Options → Decide → Communicate)

For high-stakes calls and board / org-wide communication. The human owns the decision (Rule 5); the assistant frames it, produces genuine options, and never invents a figure (Rule 13).

#### Phase 1 — Frame the decision & decision rights
Write down exactly what is being decided or announced, who owns the call, and who must be consulted versus merely informed — like setting the agenda so nobody is surprised about who actually decides.

#### Phase 2 — Gather sourced evidence & align stakeholders
Collect the real numbers, market facts, and team input behind the call; confirm every figure came from a named source rather than from memory; loop in the execs or board members who need a say.

#### Phase 3 — Develop options & scenarios (no recommendation)
Lay out two or three genuine choices with their trade-offs, risks, and knock-on effects — keeping conflicting advice visible instead of blending it into one bland answer (Rule 7).

#### Phase 4 — Decide & record rationale
The human makes the actual call, and the reasoning plus "what would make us change our mind" gets written down — so the WHY survives, not just the WHAT. This step is never automated (Rule 5).

#### Phase 5 — Craft the board deck / OKRs / org narrative
Turn the decision into the thing the audience actually reads — a board slide set, an OKR list, or an all-hands note — with every number and claim traced back to its source.

#### Phase 6 — Validate & reconcile figures
Every figure in the output ties to a source or a model cell and the totals foot; no LLM-recalled number survives. Never skipped when figures are present (Rule 13). Board material is treated as material non-public information.

#### Phase 7 — Sign-off & controlled distribution
Final read-through, explicit human approval, and only then does it go out to the board or the whole org — with a record of who approved and when.

### Definition of Done
Done = the human decided and the rationale is recorded, every figure traces to a source, and a named owner approved distribution. Legal/regulatory calls (MNPI, disclosures) are flagged to consult a human, never guaranteed.
