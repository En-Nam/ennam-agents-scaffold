### People Lifecycle Workflow (Hire → Review → Offboard)

People work follows these phases. The AI drafts and checks; a human always makes the call about a person (Rule 5). Personal data is handled on a need-to-know basis (POLICY).

#### Phase 1 — Frame & name the decision owner
Pin down exactly what people action you are taking — a hire, a review, a policy, an exit — and who makes the final human call, before touching anyone's personal data.

#### Phase 2 — Gather on a need-to-know basis
Collect only the information you actually need about the person — CV, comp band, review inputs, the relevant policy — no more, and never guess a personal detail.

#### Phase 3 — Draft against the role checklist
Write the document following the HR rulebook — competency-first JD, one interview stage per skill, 30/60/90 onboarding, structured review — so it is fair and consistent.

#### Phase 4 — Fairness & compliance check
Proofread for fairness and legal safety, not just typos — no biased or protected-class wording, no illegal questions (age, family, origin, religion, disability), every requirement justified by the job. If a legal question arises, stop and consult a human/legal (this is guidance, not legal advice).

#### Phase 5 — Human decision & written record
The manager makes the actual call — hire, rating, promotion, exit — with the reasons written down first. The AI never decides about a person, and disagreement is recorded, not averaged away (Rule 7).

#### Phase 6 — Sign-off, controlled release & retention
Get explicit human approval before anything reaches a candidate or employee, log who approved it, and set a date to delete the sensitive files when the task is done.

### Definition of Done
Done = a named human signed off, comp/headcount figures came from a supplied source (never invented — `[BAND PLACEHOLDER]` until supplied), the fairness check passed, and the decision + approval are recorded. No build or test is implied.
