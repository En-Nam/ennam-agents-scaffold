### Quick-Change Workflow (express lane)

For genuinely small, low-risk changes — a hotfix, a typo, a one-line config. If the change grows past that, switch back to the full workflow. Verify is kept even in the fast lane.

#### Phase 1 — Understand (one line)
In a single sentence, say what you are changing and why — just enough to avoid a wrong turn.

#### Phase 2 — Implement
Make the small edit.

#### Phase 3 — Verify
Confirm the change actually works — run it, test it, or eyeball the result. Never skipped.
