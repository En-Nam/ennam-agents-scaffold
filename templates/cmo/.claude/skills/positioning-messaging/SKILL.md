# Positioning & messaging playbook

Positioning is the decision about how a product should be understood, before a single campaign runs. Good positioning is specific, provable, and owns a category it can win. Use this skill any time you draft positioning or review it before it drives messaging.

## Structure (in this order)

1. **Audience** — who this is for, and — just as important — who it is not for. Name a segment, not "everyone".
2. **Problem** — the job the audience is trying to get done, and where today's alternatives fail them. State it from their point of view, not the product's.
3. **Category** — the frame of reference the audience files you under. Pick the category you can credibly win.
4. **Differentiation** — why you, not the alternatives. One or two things that are true of you and not of them.
5. **Message house** — one core message on top, 3 supporting pillars underneath, each with proof.

## Provable claims only

- Every claim should survive the question *"how do we know?"* If the answer is a number, cite its source; if it's a proof point, name it.
- Never inflate ("the leading…", "10x faster") without a source. An unprovable claim is a liability, not a benefit (Rule 13).
- Mark any figure not in hand as `[SOURCE TBD]` and surface it.

## The message house

| Layer | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Core message | The one thing you want remembered — a single sentence |
| Pillar 1-3 | The reasons to believe it, each backed by proof |
| Proof | A sourced metric, a customer outcome, or a demonstrable capability |

If a pillar has no proof, it is a hope, not a pillar — cut it or find the evidence.

## Review checklist (before positioning drives a campaign)

- The audience is a named segment, not "everyone".
- The problem is stated from the audience's point of view, not the product's.
- The category is one you can credibly win.
- Differentiation is true of you and not of the alternatives.
- Every claim is provable; every figure cites a source or is a visible `[SOURCE TBD]` (Rule 13).
- The file starts with `DRAFT` until human sign-off.
