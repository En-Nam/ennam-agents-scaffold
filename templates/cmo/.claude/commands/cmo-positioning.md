---
description: Draft positioning and messaging for a product, saved to positioning/<product>.md.
---

Usage: `/cmo-positioning <product>`

Steps:
1. If the target audience, the problem, or the competitive category are missing from the request, ask the user before drafting.
2. Read any existing files in `positioning/` to match tone and section order.
3. Draft the positioning with sections: Audience, Problem, Category, Differentiation (why us, not the alternatives), and a Message house (one core message + 3 supporting pillars, each with proof).
4. Keep claims specific and provable — mark any claim that rests on a metric with its source, or `[SOURCE TBD]` if not in hand (Rule 13).
5. Run the @positioning-messaging checklist before saving.
6. Save to `positioning/<product>.md` with a `DRAFT` marker at the top until human sign-off.
7. Surface every unproven claim and every judgment call in your handoff (Rule 12).
