---
description: Draft a content calendar for a period, saved to content/<period>.md.
---

Usage: `/cmo-content-calendar <period>`

Steps:
1. If the period's theme, channels, or cadence are missing, ask the user before drafting.
2. Read active briefs in `campaigns/` and positioning in `positioning/` so every piece ladders up to a campaign goal and stays on message.
3. Draft the calendar as a dated table: Date, Channel, Piece / Topic, Campaign it supports, Owner, Status (`PLANNED` / `DRAFT`).
4. Map each piece to a channel and a campaign goal — cut any piece that ladders up to nothing.
5. Where a branded asset is needed, note that it comes from Canva via the canva MCP; do not embed raw exports in the calendar.
6. Save to `content/<period>.md`.
7. Flag any slot with no owner or no campaign, and any external piece that will need human sign-off before it publishes (Rule 12).
