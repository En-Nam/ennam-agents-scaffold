---
description: Draft an investor update (highlights, metrics with sources, asks), saved to comms/investor-<period>.md.
---

Usage: `/ceo-investor-update <period>`

Steps:
1. If the period's metrics, key events, or asks are missing, ask the user for them or the source to pull them from before drafting.
2. Read prior updates in `comms/` to match tone, cadence, and the running narrative.
3. Draft the update with sections: **Highlights** (wins since last update), **Lowlights** (what slipped and the plan), **Metrics** (each with a baseline/prior and a cited source), **Asks** (specific, actionable requests — intros, hires, advice).
4. Cite a source for every metric; mark anything not in hand as `[SOURCE TBD]` — never invent a number (Rule 13).
5. Treat the draft as material non-public information; keep it in the repo and mark it `DRAFT` until human sign-off before it goes to investors.
6. Save to `comms/investor-<period>.md`.
7. Surface every unsourced figure and every judgment call in your handoff (Rule 12).
